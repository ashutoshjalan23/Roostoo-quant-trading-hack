# Weekly long/short momentum candidate — 2026-09-30

## Registered method

The fixed candidate adapts Grobys et al. (2025): rank a fixed basket of ten coins by simple
30-day return ending one day before the decision, long the top two, short the bottom two, and
rebalance weekly. Each side targets 50% of NAV; an entry-cost haircut keeps holdings and full
short collateral affordable without borrowing. Exposure is scaled by the sample volatility of
the last eight completed weekly portfolio returns, with 1.0x until eight returns exist. Orders
fill at the next hourly close. The model charges 0.1% taker fees and 10 bps slippage per traded
notional. Short collateral is 100%; short losses are capped at posted collateral.

The parameterization was registered before results in commit
`36fabbf6f80f32aacdaddbdce935529d1a1596b8`; the results below were generated by code commit
`adfd445c088dafc9bc52b73dd0ce0a173d4776d2`. Starting cash is $1,000,000. The 10 USDT markets
proxy USD prices. No holdout or live API orders were used.

## Interpretation and limits

This run does not support adopting this candidate as-is: the latest-year and Q3-2025-onward
results are deeply negative, while the three-year result is nearly flat with a 48% maximum
drawdown. Parameters were not changed after seeing these results. The source paper itself reports
weak and regime-dependent raw momentum results, so it is a tested design rather than a profit
guarantee; this 10-coin implementation also differs from its broader universe.

The sample uses Binance USDT spot candles as a USD proxy, a fixed current ten-coin universe,
current pair quantity steps, and modeled fees/slippage. It does not model borrow availability,
funding/interest, forced liquidation mechanics, spread variation, or a rejected short order.
Roostoo's API documents short endpoints but warns that a competition may disallow shorts; these
historical results do not establish that the current competition account will accept them. The
backtest does not send API orders. Full machine-readable output is in
[`long-short-momentum-2026-09-30.json`](long-short-momentum-2026-09-30.json).
